Daily Habits

Small, deliberate changes that compound into a different life.

Essential Practices

You don't need to throw away your phone. Start with these three boundaries and see what changes.

Phone Foyer Method

When you get home, plug your phone into a charging station in the foyer. Leave it there until you need it for a specific purpose.

Consolidated Texting

Instead of responding immediately, set aside specific times during the day to check and reply to messages.

Weekly Digital Fast

Take one day a week (like Sunday) to completely disconnect from all digital devices and reconnect with the physical world.

The 30-Day Declutter

Ready for a deeper reset? This structured process helps you rediscover what technology actually deserves a place in your life.

  • Identify

    List all optional technologies in your life (apps, streaming, social media).

  • Abstain

    Take a 30-day break from these technologies. Rediscover analog hobbies.

  • Reintroduce

    Only bring back technologies that serve a high-value purpose in your life.
