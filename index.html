Digital Minimalism

A philosophy of technology use.

Core Principles

Clutter is Costly

Cluttering your time and attention with too many devices, apps, and services creates a negative cost.

Optimization is Important

To extract the full benefit from a new technology, we must think carefully about how we use it.

Intentionality is Satisfying

Digital minimalists derive significant satisfaction from their general commitment to being more intentional.

"Digital minimalism definitively does not reject the innovations of the internet age, but instead rejects the way so many people currently engage with these tools."
— Cal Newport

Steps to Declutter

  • 01

    Define your technology rules

    Decide what is optional and what is essential.

  • 02

    Take a 30-day break

    Step away from optional technologies.

  • 03

    Reintroduce

    Bring back only what adds value to your life.

Why It Matters

Solitude

Reclaim the ability to be alone with your thoughts, free from input from other minds.

Focus

Deep work requires deep focus. Minimize distractions to maximize your creative output.

Connection

Trade low-quality digital interactions for high-quality real-world conversations.
