The Philosophy

Digital minimalism isn't about rejecting technology. It's about choosing only the technology that supports what you truly value.

Clutter is Costly

Cluttering your time and attention with too many devices, apps, and services creates a negative cost that outweighs any individual benefit.

Optimization is Important

To extract the full benefit from a new technology, we must think carefully about how and when we use it.

Intentionality is Satisfying

Digital minimalists derive deep satisfaction from their commitment to being more intentional about what they let into their lives.

The Path Forward

Change doesn't require perfection — just three deliberate steps.

  • 01

    Define your technology rules

    Decide what is optional and what is essential. Be honest about what truly serves you.

  • 02

    Take a 30-day break

    Step away from optional technologies. Rediscover what you do with unstructured time.

  • 03

    Reintroduce with intention

    Bring back only what adds real value to your life. Let go of everything else.

What You Gain

When you subtract the noise, what remains is everything that matters.

Solitude

Reclaim the ability to be alone with your thoughts, free from the constant input of other minds.

Focus

Deep work requires deep focus. Minimize distractions to unlock your best creative output.

Connection

Trade shallow digital interactions for rich, real-world conversations that actually nourish you.
