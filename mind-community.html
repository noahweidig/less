Recommended Reading

These books have guided millions toward a quieter, more intentional inner life.

Meditations

By Marcus Aurelius. Private journal entries from a Roman emperor written with no intention of publication. Two thousand years old and still unmatched.

Four Thousand Weeks

By Oliver Burkeman. A radical, liberating argument for accepting your limits — and finding meaning in the finite time you actually have.

(opens in a new tab)

The Power of Now

By Eckhart Tolle. A guide to spiritual enlightenment through present-moment awareness. One of the most transformative books on the inner life ever written.
