Recommended Reading

These books cut through financial noise and show how simplicity is the most powerful wealth-building strategy.

The Simple Path to Wealth

By JL Collins. The clearest, most accessible argument for index fund investing ever written. Originally a series of letters to his daughter.

(opens in a new tab)

Your Money or Your Life

By Vicki Robin. The foundational text of the financial independence movement. Reframes money as life energy and spending as a values question.

(opens in a new tab)

I Will Teach You to Be Rich

By Ramit Sethi. A practical, no-nonsense 6-week program to automate your finances, crush debt, and start investing — without cutting lattes.

(opens in a new tab)

Online Communities

Find others on the same path. These communities share strategies, accountability, and proof that financial simplicity works.

r/personalfinance

Millions of members sharing advice on budgeting, debt payoff, investing, and everything in between. Start with their wiki — it's excellent.

(opens in a new tab)

r/financialindependence

A community built around FIRE — Financial Independence, Retire Early. Thoughtful, data-driven discussion about building wealth and living freely.

(opens in a new tab)

Mr. Money Mustache

Pete Adeney retired at 30 on a modest salary by spending far less than he earned. His blog is one of the most influential in personal finance.
