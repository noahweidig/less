These books make the strongest case for owning less — and living more.

By Marie Kondo. The book that sparked a global movement. A practical guide to decluttering your home and, by extension, your life.

Essentialism

By Greg McKeown. The disciplined pursuit of less. A rigorous argument for doing fewer things at the highest possible level of contribution.

By Joshua Becker. A practical and personal look at how owning less creates more freedom, generosity, and meaning in everyday life.
